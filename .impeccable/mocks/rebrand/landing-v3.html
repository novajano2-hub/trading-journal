Every number has a definition.

Fees are in every figure, and each metric's formula is written down, so you can check the math yourself.

Net P&L
(exit - entry) × qty × point value - fees
Expectancy
win% × avg win - loss% × avg loss
Realized R
net P&L ÷ initial risk
Breakeven
from ≤ net ≤ to, in dollars or % of ROI
